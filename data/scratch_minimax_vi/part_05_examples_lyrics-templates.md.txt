# Các mẫu cấu trúc lời bài hát (Lyrics Templates)

## Các mô hình cấu trúc bài hát thông dụng

Trình tự sắp xếp các thẻ phân đoạn phổ biến:

**Nhạc Pop/Rock chuẩn:**
`[Intro] → [Verse] → [Pre Chorus] → [Chorus] → [Verse] → [Pre Chorus] → [Chorus] → [Bridge] → [Chorus] → [Outro]`

**Nhạc Ballad trữ tình:**
`[Intro] → [Verse] → [Verse] → [Chorus] → [Verse] → [Chorus] → [Bridge] → [Chorus] → [Outro]`

**Nhạc điện tử Electronic/Dance:**
`[Intro] → [Build Up] → [Chorus] → [Break] → [Verse] → [Build Up] → [Chorus] → [Outro]`

**Bài hát ngắn / Đơn giản:**
`[Verse] → [Chorus] → [Verse] → [Chorus] → [Outro]`

**Nhạc trường ca Progressive/Epic:**
`[Intro] → [Verse] → [Pre Chorus] → [Chorus] → [Interlude] → [Verse] → [Pre Chorus] → [Chorus] → [Bridge] → [Solo] → [Build Up] → [Chorus] → [Outro]`

## Mẫu bài hát Pop (Pop Song Template)

```
[Intro]

[Verse]
Morning light breaks through my window pane
Another day I try to start again
The coffee's cold, the silence fills the room
But something tells me change is coming soon

[Pre Chorus]
I can feel it in the air tonight
Something shifting, pulling me toward the light

[Chorus]
I'm breaking through the walls I built
Letting go of all this guilt
Every step I take is mine
I'm finally feeling fine
I'm breaking through

[Verse]
The photographs are fading on the shelf
I'm learning how to just be myself
No more hiding underneath the weight
Of everything I thought would make me great

[Pre Chorus]
I can feel it in the air tonight
Something shifting, pulling me toward the light

[Chorus]
I'm breaking through the walls I built
Letting go of all this guilt
Every step I take is mine
I'm finally feeling fine
I'm breaking through

[Bridge]
It took so long to find my voice
It took so long to make the choice
To leave the shadow and embrace the sun
My life has just begun

[Chorus]
I'm breaking through the walls I built
Letting go of all this guilt

I'm finally feeling fine
I'm breaking through

[Outro]
Breaking through...
Yeah, I'm breaking through.
```

## Mẫu bài hát Ballad trữ tình (Ballad Template)

```
[Intro]

[Verse]
Quiet streets under autumn rain
Memories whisper through the pain
Footsteps echo where we used to walk
Now there's nobody left to talk

[Verse]
A photograph in a wooden frame
Time moves on but I stay the same
Holding on to words you never said
Living in the promises instead

[Chorus]
And the rain keeps falling down tonight
Washing away the fading light
If I could turn the hands of time
Would you still be mine?

[Bridge]
Maybe someday the tears will dry
Maybe someday I'll say goodbye
Without feeling the ache inside

[Chorus]
And the rain keeps falling down tonight
Washing away the fading light
If I could turn the hands of time
Would you still be mine?

[Outro]
Still mine...
In the rain.
```
